## Capacity for Q1 and Q2 2027

- Each quarter offers **12 working weeks** per person after holidays.
- Run and support work (incidents, small changes, regulatory changes) takes about **20%** of the internal developers' time.
- Internal capacity for planned work: **≈ 58 person-weeks per quarter** (6 developers × 12 weeks × 0.8).
- Nimbus capacity: **48 person-weeks per quarter** (4 developers × 12 weeks), for Mexico only under the current contract. The renewal on 31 January 2027 can change the scope.
- The integration developer is the only person in the cell who works on MuleSoft. The Salesforce platform team can lend **one MuleSoft developer for up to 6 weeks in Q1** if the request is made before **15 December 2026**.

## Ways of working

- Scrum with two-week sprints and one backlog for the cell. Nimbus attends sprint reviews but plans its work separately.
- Architecture forum: the Technical Leads of all cells meet every two weeks, facilitated by the Architecture office. Decisions are recorded in meeting notes.

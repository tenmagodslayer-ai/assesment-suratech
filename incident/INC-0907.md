# INC-0907 — Quote journey degraded in Mexico, Colombia and Chile

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | Wednesday, 30 September 2026 (last day of the month) |
| Detected | 15:41 UTC, by the Colombian contact center |
| Declared | 15:52 UTC, as SEV3, by the on-call engineer of the Direct Digital Channel |
| Mitigated | 17:20 UTC |
| Closed | 18:20 UTC |

All times are UTC. On this date Mexico is UTC−6, Colombia UTC−5 and Chile UTC−3.

Severity scale at Meridian: **SEV1** service down for all users · **SEV2** major feature degraded for many users · **SEV3** minor feature issue for some users · **SEV4** low impact.

## What was reported

- Customers in the three countries could not get quotes, or waited a long time and then received an error. Advisors in Mexico reported the same.
- At 16:55 the Payments Integration squad paused its consumers in Mexico because the MX org reached 85% of its daily API allocation. Payment notifications in Mexico were delayed until 18:20.

## Impact measured after the incident

| Country | Quote requests that failed at `quote-xapi` (15:25–17:20) | Contact center calls about quotes (same day) |
|---|---|---|
| MX | ≈ 9,400 | 1,140 |
| CO | ≈ 1,500 | 860 |
| CL | ≈ 700 | 310 |

These figures do not include customers who gave up while waiting without receiving an error.

### Metric definitions

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `xapi_requests`, `papi_requests` | Requests received in the 5-minute interval |
| `xapi_p95_ms`, `papi_p95_ms` | 95th percentile of the time to respond, measured by each API. `quote-xapi` stops waiting at 30,000 ms |
| `xapi_5xx_pct`, `papi_error_pct` | Share of responses with a server error |
| `sf_api_calls`, `sf_requests_per_s` | Salesforce calls made by the channel's integration user in the interval, and the average per second |
| `sf_p95_ms` | 95th percentile of Salesforce response time for those calls, measured by Salesforce |
| `xapi_inflight_peak`, `xapi_queue_peak` | Highest number of requests in flight and waiting in the queue, across all replicas |
| `xapi_rejected_requests` | Requests rejected with `503` because the queue was full |
| `xapi_inflight_share_mx_pct` | Share of the in-flight requests that belonged to Mexico |
| `idp_jwt_401_pct` | Share of requests rejected by the API Manager's JWT validation |
| `sf_mx_org_daily_usage_pct` | Rolling 24-hour usage of the MX org's daily API allocation, all integrations |
